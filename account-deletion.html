CamPhotoShare Account Deletion

This page is for users who want to request deletion of their CamPhotoShare account and associated app data.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your CamPhotoShare account, send an email from the email address registered with your CamPhotoShare account.

Include your registered mobile number and write "Account Deletion Request" in the subject.

Request Account Deletion by Email

What will be deleted?

After verifying the request, CamPhotoShare will delete the account and associated app data, including account information, events, selected-photo data and support requests associated with the account.

Google Drive Data

Google Drive folders, photos and videos are not deleted automatically when a CamPhotoShare account is deleted. These files remain in the user's connected Google Drive.

Processing

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed after verification of account ownership.
