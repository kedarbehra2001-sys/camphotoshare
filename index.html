About CamPhotoShare

CamPhotoShare is a photo sharing application designed for photographers to organize and share event photos with their clients.

Photographers can create events, upload photos, and share their event gallery with clients through a simple QR code or private link for effortless online album photo selection.

Key Features

Smart Album Photo Selection

Clients open their private gallery link, browse through high-resolution photos, and select their favorites for album design. Photographers receive the finalized shortlist instantly.

Event-Based Sharing

Organize photos according to weddings, events, and other photography projects systematically.

Google Drive

Connect Google Drive seamlessly to store, sync, and manage your event photos in the cloud.

QR Code Sharing

Share complete event galleries instantly using simple scan-to-view QR codes for guests.

Easy Client Access

Clients can access shared event photos and shortlist their favorites without creating a separate account.
