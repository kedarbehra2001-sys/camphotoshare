Frequently Asked Questions

What is CamPhotoShare and who is it designed for?

CamPhotoShare is an event photo sharing and album selection platform built specifically for wedding and event photographers, studios, and organizers. It replaces manual pen drives and handwritten photo numbers with instant QR-code mobile galleries and automated desktop selection workflows.

Where are the original photos stored? Does CamPhotoShare charge monthly cloud storage fees?

No. CamPhotoShare works on a "Bring Your Own Storage" model using your existing Google Drive. Your high-resolution files stay secure in your own cloud account, completely eliminating expensive monthly hosting fees and server lock-in.

How does the 1-Click Windows desktop sync work for Photoshop and album design?

When clients select their favorite photos on their mobile browser and submit, the CamPhotoShare Windows desktop app matches those file names and automatically copies the raw or high-res photos from your local hard disk into a dedicated "Selected" folder in just one click.

Do wedding guests or clients need to install an app to view photos?

No app installation is required for clients or guests. They simply scan the event QR code or click the web gallery link to browse photos, mark favorites, and submit selections from any modern mobile browser.
