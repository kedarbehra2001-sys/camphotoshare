About CamPhotoShare

CamPhotoShare is a photo sharing application designed for photographers to organize and share event photos with their clients.

Photographers can create events, upload photos, and share their event gallery with clients through a simple QR code.

Features

Event-Based Sharing

Organize photos according to weddings, events and other photography projects.

Google Drive

Connect Google Drive to store and manage event photos.

QR Code Sharing

Share an event gallery easily using a QR code.

Easy Client Access

Clients can access shared event photos without creating a separate account.
