Pen-Drive Aur 6 Mahine Ka Intezaar Khatam: Kaise Ek Indian Photographer Ne Banaya Yeh Smart App

Published on CamPhotoShare Blog | By Kedar Behra

Agar aap bhi ek wedding photographer hain, toh aap yeh dard acche se jante honge—shaadi khatam hone ke baad photo selection ke liye mahino tak client ke pichhe bhagna aur hard-disk me IMG files dhoondna.

Shaadi ka season khatam hote hi ek photographer ka asli sangharsh shuru hota hai. Din bhar shooting karne ke baad, raat ko 2 baje studio me baith kar hazaron photos ke beech me se client ki pasand ki photos dhoondhna kisi saza se kam nahi hota.

Ek Photographer Ne Hi Kyun Banaya Yeh App?

Main khud ek wedding photographer hoon aur apne studio me roz ki inhi problems ko face karta tha. Mujhe samajh aaya ki aaj ke digital zamane me bhi agar hum purane tarike use kar rahe hain, toh hamara adha waqt sirf management me nikal jata hai.

CamPhotoShare Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Pura Workflow)

  • 1. QR Code se Instant Sharing: Venue par guests bina app download kiye QR scan karke apne candid shots dekh aur download kar sakte hain.
  • 2. Mobile Par Selection: Client apne phone se aaram se favorite photos par ❤️ tap karke apne selections final karte hain.

🔥 Magic Feature: 1-Click Hard-Disk Auto-Sync

Jaise hi client udhar se apna selection complete karta hai, aapka kaam minto ka reh jata hai:

  • Aapko bas apne Windows desktop app me hard-disk ke andar original folders select karne hain.
  • Uske baad bas 'Shortlist' button par click karna hai.
  • Button dabate hi computer apne aap saari selected photos ka ek alag folder me saaf-saaf copy bana deta hai! Ab ek-ek karke photo dhoondne ki zaroorat nahi padti.
